The problem

Same water, a different answer for each vehicle

Every monsoon, underpasses and low roads in Indian cities fill with water and then drain over the next half hour to several hours. Navigation apps don’t know how deep the water is, whether your vehicle can get through, or when it will clear.

25 cm of water is routine for a bus, risky for a hatchback and dangerous for a scooter. JalMarg treats passability as a property of the (road segment, vehicle class) pair, forecasts it ahead in time, and lets the router choose when to go as well as where: wait for the underpass to drain, or take the detour.

How it works

From a video frame to “wait 18 min, or detour +11 min”

  1. Ingest

    Camera adapters read CCTV, video files or citizen photos and tag each frame with a road segment and time.

  2. Perceive

    YOLO11-seg segments the water and finds wheels, kerbs and people of known size.

  3. Measure

    Each reference object gives a depth with its own error; they are fused into one h ± σ.

  4. Track

    A Kalman filter per segment merges all sources, weighted by uncertainty and reliability.

  5. Forecast

    A physics prior corrected by a GRU predicts depth quantiles for the next hour or two.

  6. Decide

    Time-to-passable per vehicle feeds a time-dependent router that compares waiting with detouring.

Depth from reference objects

h_r = f_r · L_r σ_r² = (L_r·σ_f)² + (f_r·σ_L)² ĥ = Σ(h_r/σ_r²) / Σ(1/σ_r²) σ_ĥ² = 1 / Σ(1/σ_r²)

f is the submerged fraction of an object of real height L, measured where the water mask cuts an ellipse fitted to the wheel.

Forecast and decision

dh/dt = α·R(t) − k·(h − h∞) physics prior + GRU residual, pinball loss τ ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 0.9} t*_v = min{ t ≥ now : q₀.₉(h(t)) < d_v − m }

R(t) is rainfall. Using the 90th percentile means JalMarg says “passable” only when it is confident.

Deep learning results

The hybrid forecaster beats both baselines

Three forecasters trained on 3,000 synthetic rise-and-drain events (7,200 training and 1,800 test windows, split by event so no event appears in both). Lower error is better. The unsafe rate counts time-to-passable decisions that said “go” while the true depth was still above the vehicle’s limit.

Object-oriented analysis and design

Seven actors, twelve use cases, eight patterns

Road-segment state machine

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Dry
  Dry --> Wet: rain > r0
  Wet --> Dry: dry for 30 min
  Wet --> Rising: h > 5 cm
  Rising --> Receding: dh/dt < 0 sustained
  Receding --> Rising: rain resumes
  Receding --> Wet: h < 5 cm
  Rising --> Unknown: no data > 20 min
  Receding --> Unknown: no data > 20 min
  Unknown --> Wet: new observation

Impassable is not a state. A segment is in one state at a time, but passability depends on the vehicle, so it is a query: segment.time_to_passable(vehicle). Unknown is treated as impassable for vehicles whose safe depth is under 30 cm.

Invention disclosure

What we ask the IPR cell to protect

PriorityNovel featureHow it differs from current systems
1Vehicle-class-specific time-to-passabilityEarliest time the 90th-percentile forecast drops below each class’s safe depth, where others give one open/closed flag for the present moment.
2Wait-or-detour routing on vehicle-specific opening timesA time-dependent search may wait at a node for an edge to open; conventional routers only remove or penalise flooded edges.
3Physics-constrained neural recession forecasterMetric depth quantiles from video-derived series, where CCTV methods give relative trends or coarse danger classes.
4Reliability-weighted multi-source state estimator with a fail-safe stateWeights cameras and citizen reports by σ and reliability; missing data means unsafe, never clear.
